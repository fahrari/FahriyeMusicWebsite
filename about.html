introducing me <3

I grew up between two musical worlds with both Turkish songs and western pop on my CDs and iPod. Both live in me, and both show up in my music, sometimes on their own, sometimes fused.

I'm Fahriye (Fah-ree-yeh), a multi-threat recording artist currently based in Boston, MA. I grew up in San José, Bay Area, CA, and my roots are Turkish + German.

"Maddy knew who she was from a very early age."

Euphoria HBO · S1E5

I'm a vocalist, songwriter, producer, performer, and dancer whose influences span Beyoncé to Hadise. I've been songwriting since I was 16, debuted with "Move" on my 20th birthday, and released my debut EP GENESIS in 2026.

My sound is unique, fresh, and fully me — grounded in western pop, traditional Turkish music, and everything in between. There is nothing I love more than being an artist with a powerful creative vision.
