Biography

Fahriye (Fah-ree-yeh) is a recording artist currently based in Boston, MA. She grew up in San José, Bay Area, CA, with Turkish and German roots — her Turkish heritage that lives in every track she makes.

If there's one thing she wants you to take from her music, it's that power and vulnerability can coexist and are in fact necessary to feed the other. Even when her instrumentals sound powerful and you can feel the bass in every part of your body, her lyrics tell the story from the perspective of a young twenty-something year-old processing it. One who feels everything deeply.

A multi-threat vocalist, songwriter, producer, performer, and dancer Fahriye creates pop music whose influences span Beyoncé to Hadise. She has been songwriting consistently since she was 16 years old, debuted with "Move" on her 20th birthday (July 4th, 2025), and is releasing her debut EP, [REDACTED], in 2026. Her next single "RESIST" is a first foray into love/crush songs, steeped in Turkish musical elements, stacked harmonies, and a dramatic intro, arriving October 23rd, 2026.

Her sound is unique, fresh, and fully her own while staying grounded in western pop music, traditional Turkish music, and pop that infuses middle-eastern elements into its sound. She has also been performing and dancing for as long as she can remember, striving to be an artist who constantly raises the bar for herself: never only in one place, appreciating what she's accomplished, but always looking forward to how she can keep getting even better. There is nothing she loves more than being a vocalist, songwriter, producer, performer, dancer, and overall someone with a powerful creative vision.
