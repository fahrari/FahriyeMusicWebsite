Single · Oct 31 2025

Make or Break

Make or Break was an incredibly experimental track for me. It's a dark pop track about wanting to enjoy the moment. I wrote it at a time where there was a lot of stress coming at me from what felt like all sides of my life. I was having sleepless nights and I wanted a track that I could dance to and make me feel free. I love the build-up through the song, leading to a very dancey ending. Halloween also fell on a Friday in 2025, so it felt right to release this song that day.

Single · Jul 4 2025

Move

Move is my debut single about partying with your partner and even with every temptation that exists in a club setting, you just want to let loose with them. The click clack sounds you hear in the background is the sound of Turkish spoons and the pre-chorus includes a bağlama. It was my very first time incorporating Turkish elements into a song. I also released this song on my 20th birthday, as it had fallen on a Friday in 2025, because what better way to start a decade than debuting your music career.
